BackIntel five-minute showcase

This is a recorded synthetic business case. Actual Jev, CatBoost and TabICLv2 executions are retained. Navigating the package makes no new model calls.

0:00-0:40 — Show the problem. A support lead reads reports, checks queue measurements, assembles evidence and repeats that work after corrections.
0:40-1:20 — Show the original Access report and the actual Jev interpretation. A message saying login failed is evidence about the report, not proof of its cause.
1:20-2:05 — Compare CatBoost and TabICLv2, facts alone and facts plus Jev, against a baseline. The six synthetic selection cases do not prove customer accuracy.
2:05-2:50 — Show the corrected Accounts report and refreshed packet. Accounts is about 26.4%; Access is about 78.7%. Both are experimental estimates.
2:50-3:40 — Open the source evidence and inspect the retained versions. Explain what is verified, estimated and unknown.
3:40-4:20 — Show the saved synthetic human review. React and the optional Python interface use the same local review API.
4:20-5:00 — Show replay and limits. All 90 jobs were reused. Savings, revenue, customer accuracy and full operating costs remain to be measured.
